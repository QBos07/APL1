# 6.2 — Add individual part/unit schema, repository, and core use cases

**Status:** Implemented  
**Goal:** Track identifiable physical units separately from their reusable part type.

## Data policy

- A unit belongs to one team and one part type; it has a stable ID and optional user-entered serial number, part number, condition/status label, and notes.
- Serial and part numbers are genuinely optional. A unit with no serial remains a distinct row by stable ID.
- Do not infer that an unassigned unit is available inventory or that a removed unit is in stock.
