# 6.7 — Link a replacement cost to one ledger transaction

**Status:** Implemented  
**Goal:** Associate a user-recorded replacement cost with its actual ledger entry without duplicating or recounting money.

## Link policy

- Add an explicit nullable `transaction_id` foreign key to the replacement lifecycle event; do not use a generic polymorphic relationship table.
- A replacement operation links to at most one expense transaction. In v1 a transaction can be linked to at most one lifecycle event, avoiding ambiguous allocation across multiple replacements.
- The link is contextual only: ledger reports continue to count the transaction once. Parts views read the linked transaction and do not create an independent cost amount.
- Corrections/reversals are resolved through the ledger's original-transaction correction chain; do not attach the same correction entries as additional part costs.
