# 2.20 — Anchor the team switcher at the bottom of the tab rail

**Status:** Complete (2026-11-24)  
**Goal:** Make team identity and switching available from every view without occupying a primary tab.

## Work list

- [x] Move the team switcher control from the Teams screen/navigation flow to a persistent control at the bottom of the vertical tab rail.
- [x] Show the active team name (or “No active team”) and a switch/dropdown affordance.
- [x] Open a team-selection dialog/list from that control; show active marker and archived teams only through an explicit include-archived choice.
- [x] Mirror the active team identity in the bottom status bar and let activating the status-bar team indicator open the same switcher.
- [x] Keep the Teams tab for team management/detail, distinct from switching the active workspace.
- [x] Ensure highlighting or hovering never switches teams; explicit activation selects and persists.
- [x] Preserve the previously selected body tab when a team is switched.

## Acceptance checks

- [x] The switcher remains at the bottom of the tab rail across all primary views.
- [x] Active team is also identified in the status bar on every view.
- [x] The rail control and status indicator open the same team picker behavior.
- [x] Switching is explicit, persists by stable team ID, and shows the fallback notice when applicable.
- [x] Team switching returns to the same body tab with the new team's context.
- [x] Mouse and keyboard can open, navigate, and select in the switcher; browsing alone performs no write.
