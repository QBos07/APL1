# 2.20 — Anchor the team switcher at the bottom of the tab rail

**Status:** Not started  
**Goal:** Make team identity and switching available from every view without occupying a primary tab.

## Work list

- [ ] Move the team switcher control from the Teams screen/navigation flow to a persistent control at the bottom of the vertical tab rail.
- [ ] Show the active team name (or “No active team”) and a switch/dropdown affordance.
- [ ] Open a team-selection dialog/list from that control; show active marker and archived teams only through an explicit include-archived choice.
- [ ] Mirror the active team identity in the bottom status bar and let activating the status-bar team indicator open the same switcher.
- [ ] Keep the Teams tab for team management/detail, distinct from switching the active workspace.
- [ ] Ensure highlighting or hovering never switches teams; explicit activation selects and persists.
- [ ] Preserve the previously selected body tab when a team is switched.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] The switcher remains at the bottom of the tab rail across all primary views.
- [ ] Active team is also identified in the status bar on every view.
- [ ] The rail control and status indicator open the same team picker behavior.
- [ ] Switching is explicit, persists by stable team ID, and shows the fallback notice when applicable.
- [ ] Team switching returns to the same body tab with the new team's context.
- [ ] Mouse and keyboard can open, navigate, and select in the switcher; browsing alone performs no write.

## Dependencies / boundaries

- Depends on [2.16](2.16-redesign-layout-geometry.md), [2.19](2.19-vertical-view-tabs.md), and completed team persistence/switching ([2.9](2.9-active-team-setting.md), [2.10](2.10-team-switcher-screen.md)).
