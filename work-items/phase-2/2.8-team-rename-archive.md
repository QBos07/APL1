# 2.8 — Add team rename and archive operations

**Status:** Done (2026-11-24)
**Goal:** Let users maintain team identity and archive a workspace without destroying its records.

## Implementation notes (2026-11-24)

- Repository update preserves the stable ID, created timestamp, and archive
  state while changing name/notes and updating record metadata. Rename reuses
  the 2.7 normalization/uniqueness rules and leaves the stored row unchanged on
  failure.
- Archive sets archive state/timestamp without deleting the team or notes, is
  idempotent on repeat, and refuses to archive the last non-archived team.
- If the archived team was active, the archive transaction selects the
  oldest-created remaining active team (stable ID tie-breaker) and reports the
  switch; otherwise the active selection is untouched. History/detail remains
  retrievable. Tests: `test_team_service.c`, `test_team_repository.c`,
  `test_team_isolation.c`.
