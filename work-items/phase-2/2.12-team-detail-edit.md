# 2.12 — Build the team detail and edit screen

**Status:** Done (2026-11-24)
**Goal:** Provide a readable team profile view and safe editing for the fields supported in the team schema.

## Implementation notes (2026-11-24)

- The detail view shows name, honest notes (empty = "not recorded"), active or
  archived status, created/updated/archived metadata, and placeholder links for
  later People/Cars/Money sections without creating or summarising data.
- Edit reuses normalization/uniqueness and update behavior, preserves the team
  ID, retains edits on validation/duplicate errors, and focuses the relevant
  field. Cancel (with a dirty-form confirmation) leaves the saved team unchanged.
- Archive requires confirmation, is hidden/explained when the team is the last
  active team, and returning to the list preserves the current selection/filter.
  Tests: `test_team_service.c` and the archive smoke workflow.
